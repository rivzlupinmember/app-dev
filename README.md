# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man

**Genre:** Action, Adventure, Superhero  
**Created by:** Stan Lee
**Main Character:** *Peter Parker / Spider-Man*

---

## Why I Love the Spider-Man Movies
- They show how anyone can be a hero, even a regular teenager.  
- The mix of **action, humor, and emotion** makes every film exciting.  
- Each version of Spider-Man brings something unique:
  - **Tobey Maguire** – the classic and emotional hero  
  - **Andrew Garfield** – the witty and modern Spider-Man  
  - **Tom Holland** – the young and energetic version  

---
### My Favorite Lessons
1. *With great power comes great responsibility.*  
2. Never give up even when the world doubts you.  
3. Being a hero is not about fame, but about doing what’s right.

---
> “No matter how buried it gets, or how lost you feel, you must promise me that you will hold on to hope.”  
> *Aunt May, Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021)*

---
### Fun Facts
- Spider-Man first appeared in **Amazing Fantasy #15 (1962)**.  
- The character has appeared in over **10 live-action and animated films**.  
- The Spider-Verse movies won **Academy Awards** for Best Animated Feature.
